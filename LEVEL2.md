# Level 2 — the owner's brief (verbatim, 2026-10-05)

Status and stages: HANDOVER.md, "LEVEL 2 PROJECT". Way of working the owner asked for: break it into stages,
the main session as project manager with subagents doing one stage at a time; subagents wait for approval of
screenshots of any art/looks, with rounds of changes; the PM presents them to the owner and relays feedback.

> - a level that certain areas seem to turn the gun torch off. It is shrouded in complete darkness and silhouette. A thick spun silk coating covers the background layers, causing the hologram layer to be diffused, and causing stark black silhouettes of everything in the caves and tunnels. Entering the darkness without a source of light quickly gets you killed by what's in there. Firing bullets that illuminate areas will show quick flashes of the ground moving somehow. If any fire were to come with you into the darkness, by torch or flame thrower or explosions, all enemies within a custom dev radius flee from the fire source. In the short cast light from the flames you see hundreds of indescribable aliens scattering like cockroaches when a light has been turned on. They use BOIDS algorithm of separation, alignment and coheaion to flow away in packs. They can traverse any part of the background silk. Explosions are the only things that can destroy parts of this background silk layer. The little aliens bodies are the size of the players helmet, but they have 3 very thin spider legs equally placed and aimed outwards about 3 times its body width. Inside its body shape, taking up almost all of its body is a single eye ball, with a black dot for the pupil which darts around until a player is in agro distance then locks on starring at the player. It's movement when roaming can be a slightly faster version of the spider. But it doesn't attack unless in darkness. It flees from light (but electrical ones don't work in this zone), so you have to corner them to eliminate them.
>
> Occasionally an alien from the dark zone will be found roaming outside of its zone and as soon as it sees you it sprints away to its closest dark zone. In these scenarios they should be shown as all black.
>
> The other patches of the level without this zone is just void of living plants or animals, it's a wasteland of bones and skulls sticking out of the ground. Some areas have huge explosion holes cut out with scorching from things having been burnt just beyond the explosion radius. The amount of war and destruction damage done to the environment is an expanding gradient outwards of the first established dark zone areas. With a dev control set max distance, I want to create a gradient outward from the edges of the dark zone and into the rest of the map, and decorate it with an amount of destruction based on a bezieir dev control where Y is destruction amount and X is distance from dark zone. The destruction could be done two ways, try to add it to the terrain yourself manually, or you could just set off explosions along the large pathways. Just scatter points across the map with the density of the points scattered being based on the distance from dark zone gradient, with anything inside terrain and within a distance of terrain removed. Give dev controls for number of explosions, percentage of them that causes fire, size random min and max, additional position randomisation, additional scale modifier bezieir tool where Y is scale mult 0 to 2 and X is dist from dark zone.
> This decorate by distance system could be created as a decoration tool first, to be reused another time. So keep that in mind when building.
>
> The level design that this destruction and dark zone has destroyed sections of is a tomb of a once brilliant ancient civilisation. Signs of organisation, architecture, worship in every geometrically carved out room, small or large. There are no places of untamed wilds, only skeletal remains and/or forgotten furniture, belongings, shrines, meeting areas, unexplainable rooms, all covered in room unique decorations.
>
> I'd say do this first, then cut out the dark zone from it, place in the dark zone, then apply damage. Wait until it settles (speed up if needed), drop loot through the cave at the same spawn points you would have if you'd spawned half a levels worth of enemies through the non- dark zone parts (no green crystals though). Then in the center of the dark zones are prizes you have to reach. Each dark zone would have one of the following: A big stash of gold nuggets (maybe 1000-2000), 4-6 red crystals, or 1-3 green crystals.
> Then the level is ready to play.
>
> You could hide the time it takes to build it behind the teleporter animation before it reveals it. But let's not let it take more than 5 seconds.
