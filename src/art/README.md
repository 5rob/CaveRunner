Creature sprites live with their creature (`creatures/`). Everything draws at world scale.

## Rules

- `drawProp` runs **before** the fog; `propGlow` adds light **after** it, only where `fogLit` says
  the ground is seen — except the eyes, which fade as you come close (`eyesAlpha`).
- The hand torch (**archived** since v0.0.145, `HAND_TORCH` in `game/systems/player.js`: the gun light took
  its place, `render/light.js`; the code stays for creatures later): `drawFlame`'s lean is a spring toward "opposite your velocity" (`W.leanX`/`leanY`,
  `stepTorch`); its halo, second light and the wall sconces are drawn after the fog with `lighter`,
  so the map lighting is untouched. Brightness follows `flick` (`world/README.md`).
- Ground guns glow (`drawGunGlow`, before the gun sprite) unless `q.old` — set on the gun you swap
  out in `GunSwap`, and kept by the save.
- A gun's sprite colour is `gunAccent` (its level colour; starter guns keep their family colour).
- **Zones by noise (Auto, v1.0.50):** `titleZoneLook(wx, y, B)` (used by `titleZoneAt`): in a level x is pushed by a domain-warped fbm field and islands (knobs `autoZoneWarp/Scale/Rag/Isle`), so a zone's skin, moss and bricks curve across the height; pads, arena and exit keep their own. Since v1.0.51 the caves' shapes follow it too: `zoneOpenAt(wx, y, B)` (pure, `autoZoneOpen` 1) opens another zone's own air in rock (not pads, arena, blocked zones, a mine's look, nor under the floor); `titleRoof` is the roof as carved; plants, webs and pads pick their zone by `titleZoneLook` at the roof / floor. Tall levels keep runners above `S.vh - PH`; `S.huddle` (auto/level.js, after the boss) keeps flyers to the team's middle height. Blocked zones and gameplay stay x-based.
- **Fire climbs (Auto, v1.0.53):** a burning plant or silk strand is alight from `p.lit` (a front from the root, climbing `autoVineSpread` × its burn) to the tip; in a level its whole stretch calls `spreadFrom(S, x, y, J, P)` with `autoFireJump`/`autoFireJumpP`. The title: only the tip, `TITLE_JUMP`/`TITLE_JUMPP`. Suite `auto-vinefire`, shots `tools/vinefireshots.js`. **Silk (v1.0.61):** a burning strand (`st` silk) reads the `autoSilk*` knobs in place of the vines'; `behind(S, d)` culls nothing in the fire sim (`S.lab`); `cutWeb` hangs no strand from an end on the floor. **Fire particles (Auto, v1.0.60):** in Auto's rules `flameAt` / `fireSmoke` / `titleJet` make `fx` particles (`fxPart`: `u` its colour shift, `a` its opacity, `g` smoke growth) from the `firefx` knobs; titledraw colours them by `fxFlGrad`/`fxSmGrad` and `fxFlAlpha`/`fxSmAlpha`. **Aim (Auto, v1.0.59):** `titleAimPick(S, r)` the runner's target: in a level within `autoAimR`, not in the right-hand `autoAimEdge` strip or past it, up to `autoAimLeft` off the left, the boss anywhere; the title: `TITLE_AIM` and well on screen. Suite `auto-aim`. **Ropes (unreleased, waiting on the owner):** a burnt-off piece falls as a loose rope (`ropeMake`, `ropeStep`: verlet points `p.rp`/`p.rq`, links `p.rs`, stopped by rock; drawn through `p.tl` like a hanging tail), draping and burning away where it lies. **Burn through (v1.0.57):** in Auto's rules a burning vine or strand burns through at `autoBurnCut` a fire tick; the piece below drops with `rot` / `rv` (spin up to `autoBurnSpin`), landing on its lower end (`titleTumbleLow`). **Leaves (v1.0.56):** in Auto's rules (`autoOn`: a level or the fire sim) every point of a burning stretch, its leaves (`LEAFY` plants) too, lights fuel cells within `autoLeafReach` at `autoLeafLight` a tick; `titleIgnite(S, sx, y, r, chance)` takes screen x. **Three states (v1.0.55):** unburnt to `p.lit`, burning `p.lit..p.ch`, charred and crumbling `p.ch..p.len` (`autoVineAsh`); `titledraw.js` draws the burning stretch alight all along (the title: `p.ch = p.len`, only the tip); the climb spurts by `titleNoise` (`autoVineJitter`).
- **Round it before sawing (Auto, v1.0.55):** `startDig` asks `navAround` first: `rockNormal(S, r, dx, dy)` (the rock's average normal 9 ahead, a ring of 24 at radius 10) · (1, 0) facing back under `autoNavCut`: over by `jetOver` (open air above), under with a gap below; else, or after `autoNavGive` s of trying (`r.navT`), the saw. A dry player in rock mid-air drops to land instead. Suite `auto-exitnav`.
- **Deeper is harder (Auto, v1.0.52):** `titleCarve` with its rim (a blast, a pit) breaks cells within r × `deepK(S, y, wx)`: less toward the level's hard line (`S.lvl.hardY`, v1.0.54: the bottom edge, raised to the exit pad's floor; else `vh − autoHardEdge`), none in its last `autoHardEdge`, tall levels only (knobs `autoHardDeep`/`autoHardEdge`). The saw (rim off) cuts as ever. Suite `auto-deep`.
- **Tall levels (Auto):** `TitleCam` takes `view` (the screen's height when shorter than `by`; `camClamp` keeps the view inside 0..by) and `pack` (`camPack(C, S, e)`: ease y toward the team's middle height; `camStep` does it when nobody is locked). `bandK`, `TITLE_PACKY`: see src/auto/README.md, Tall levels.
- **Trajectory mods in the scene (Part A):** scenegun.js `shotsOf` carries `orbit`/`spiral`/`boomer`/`pong` (lifetime × auto/traj.js `trajLife`); titlescene.js `stepShots` moves them by `trajStep` round the caster's middle, keeps the light trail `s.ptr` (`trajTrail`), and lets an orbit (`trOrbRock`) or a returning boomerang (`trBoRock`) pass through rock; titledraw.js draws the trail and glow (`trajLook`).
