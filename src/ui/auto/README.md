# ui/auto/ — layer 6: CaveRunner Auto's screens (branch `autobattler`)

React for the auto-battler (`AUTOBATTLER.md`). The pure parts are in `src/auto/` (the run model, the save);
this folder only shows them and takes the taps.

## Files

- `AutoScreen.js` — (stage 2) the play screen, top to bottom (`.auto`, a flex column, CSS in `style.css`):
  - **play area** `.aplay` (55% of the height): the hub (stage 3a: `auto/hub.js` `hubScene`, opening on the teleport-in, `warm: 0`; the camera follows player 1, `C.w = HUB_W`, pinch out to the whole row) on `.aplaycvs` through `ui/scenecanvas.js`; `hubScene(..., run.tier)` for the prices; the scene kept in a ref for the buttons: the pill stick calls `hubStick` (free roam), A calls `hubExit`
    `runScene` (pinch, drag, tap a player to follow), `titleScene(…, { runners: run.players.length })`;
    ⏸ (`.pausebtn`) opens `ui/pause.js` `PauseMenu` (label `Tier n`; Save / Exit write the run) and holds the scene.
    **Stage 4a (test only, till 4b wires it):** `window.__AUTO_LEVEL = seed` makes `make` open a level instead (`auto/level.js levelScene`, default camera); in a level the pill stick's sideways push (past `DEAD`) goes to `levelHold` (right hurries, left slows, never stops; up does nothing). `tools/levelshots.js` uses it.
  - **context nav** `.anav`: 4 circles (`.anavc`, `data-player`), a player's colour ring (`TITLE_COLS`) and number
    for each player in the run (`.on`), an empty dark circle for a locked one. **Stage 8a:** `NavRow` draws `auto/nav.js` `navRow` (state `nav`, `navStart`): circles (`.anavc`; `data-player` at the top, `data-open` = what a tap opens) for choices (a player's ring and number, the menu glyphs 🔫 🧑‍🚀 ⭐ 📈, a gun's `GunIcon`, the exo glyphs), square tiles (`.aslot.anavs`, the bag's `ItemIcon`, empty ones dim) for slots. Below the top the row's edge is the tapped player's colour; `.sel` (white ring) marks the active gun, and at the top the last tapped player. A slot row (`.anav.slots`) starts at the left and scrolls sideways (`overflow-x: auto`, `pan-x`). `.anav` is a fixed 66px at every level (the layout never jumps); `data-level` on it. Stats (stage 9): `StatsRow`, two canvases (`.astats`, `.astatg.dmg` red damage dealt, `.astatg.hp` green health; `paintGraph`, the old Bag `GunFire` graph's line scaled to its peak, redrawn every 250 ms) from the nav player's `levelState(S).meters[p]` through `auto/meters.js` `meterTail`; flat in the hub (no meters). A tap cycles the span 5 s → 15 s → 30 s (`nextSpan`; `.astatspan` small top right; `data-span`; each canvas has `data-n` samples, `data-peak`).
  - **bag** `.abag`: `BAG_SLOTS` (70) `.aslot`s, 7 across, scrolling up and down (`touch-action: pan-y`). A full
    slot (`.full.k-<kind>`): a gun's sprite (`GunIcon`), a mod's `MODS` glyph, an exo mod's `STAT_PERKS` glyph and
    tint with its tier (I–V), a perk's glyph, gold ● / red ◆ / green ◆; the count (`.acount`) when more than one,
    and always for gold and gems.
    In a level (stage 6 part 2) the screen passes `run` to `levelScene` (its 7th argument): kills' drops go into `run.bag`; the 200 ms poll reads `L.bagV` (+1 per item taken) into a state so the bag redraws while the level runs.
  - **buttons** `.abtns`: B (red pill), the **pill stick** (`PillStick`, `.apill`, owner after stage 4a: the old `<` `>` circles' height and 23px roundness, filling their space), A (green pill). The pill stick works like the old game's left thumbstick (`ui/hud.js` `Stick`): pointer capture, a knob (`.apillknob`, orange edge while it reads "up", above its neighbours) that follows the finger out to `PILL_REACH` (54 px, the old 150 px stick's reach) every way, past the pill's edges (owner, feedback round 1: the old stick's maths exactly; before, dy by the pill's small half-height stole the sideways push); its state `PillState { active, nx, ny, mag, dx, dy }`: mag the finger's distance / `PILL_REACH` clamped to 1, nx/ny its direction; letting go resets it. `touch-action: none`; works with a mouse. B goes back one level in the nav (`navBack`; nothing at the top). The nav runs in the hub and in a level, never pausing.
  - The run: `loadAutoRun()`, or `newRun()` saved at once. `where` (hub or level): runScene's `next` swaps at `hubLeft` (to the run's level, healed) and `levelDone` (home, `levelCleared`); saved at each swap.

Tests: `tests/browser/auto-screen.test.js` (stage 8a: the nav, every level, B, the height, in a level too). Nav pictures: `node tools/navshots.js` (`tests/build/autoshots/nav-*.png`). ## Rules

- Phone first (412×880, touch); every gesture works with a mouse too.
- The title menu's colours (`.titlemenu`, `.tslot`: dark purple panels, orange edge), not new ones.
- Slots in scrolling areas are `pan-y`; anything you drag gets `touch-action: none` (`.grab`, as the old Bag).
- No game logic here: moves on the run go through `src/auto/run.js`.

**Path blocked (stage 5b):** in a level, when no gun in play can clear the rock ahead (`auto/clear.js`), the pilot stops the team and `AutoScreen` shows `.ablocked` ("Path blocked", pulsing, `style.css`) over the play area; it reads `levelState(S).blocked` every 200 ms. Under `__TEST_TITLE` the level scene is on `window.__autoScene` (for `tools/clearshots.js`).

**LEVEL CLEARED (stage 7):** `runScene`'s `over` paints `art/cleared.js` `clearedText` while `levelClearedAge(S)` ≥ 0 (top at 14% of the play area): the title's letters (`titleLetter`) drop in one by one with bounces, a spark burst and a ring at each landing, a shine sweep, the title's bob, a fade over the last 0.5 s. Knobs Dev → Auto → "Auto: level" (`autoClearT`, `autoClearWait`). Pictures: `tools/clearedshots.js`.

**Enemies (stage 6 part 1):** the level gets `run.tier` (`levelScene`'s 6th argument). While the arena's boss lives, `AutoScreen` shows `.abossbar` (its name and a red bar, `style.css`) across the top of the play area, from `auto/enemies.js` `levelBoss(S)`, polled with the blocked hint (200 ms). Every player fallen (`levelLost(S)`): `next` takes the team home with `levelFailed(run)` (healed, tier unchanged) and saves.
- `icons.js` (stage 8a, owner rounds): `PixIcon` (pixel maps `NAV_ICONS`: the menu's guns/exo/perks/stats, the exo kinds hp/speed/jet/carrot; also the bag's exo mods), `HelmetIcon` (a player's helmet, `art/sprites.js` `helmetIcon` through `pixelSprite`), `GlyphIcon` (a mod's/perk's/gold's/gem's glyph: drawn big, its painted pixels found, scaled to fill a `GLYPH_PX` grid, alpha thresholded; `.apixg` 76% of the tile, `image-rendering: pixelated`, no glow). No emoji in the nav (owner).

**Drag and drop (stage 8b):** a bag tile or a nav slot tile takes a press (`down` in `AutoScreen`, window pointer listeners, mouse and touch alike). Only the **grab handle** `.agrab` (a circle round the icon's centre, radius `DEV.autoGrab` × the tile width, Dev → Auto → "Auto: bag", default 0.35) is `touch-action: none`: a press there that moves past 6 px (`MOVE`) is a drag; anywhere else the tile stays `pan-y` (the bag scrolls; a nav slot row `pan-x`) and the browser's pointercancel ends the press with no card. A press released within 6 px is a **tap**: the item's card (`CardPop`, portalled into the body: `ModCard`/`GunCard`/`PerkCard` from `ui/cards.js`, an exo mod's own `ExoCard` from `STAT_PERKS`; gold and gems have none; the shade closes it). While dragging: the `.aghost` tile above the finger, the source faded (`.lift`), a target it fits lit green (`.drop`): bag → a gun's mod slots (nav at gun, mods), the 4 gun circles (nav at guns, `data-gslot`, guns), a category's slots (nav at cat, its exo mods only), perk slots (nav at perks, non-stat perks); a nav slot → the bag (unfit). The move goes through `auto/run.js` (`fitMod` … `unfitPerk`) and saves; anything else springs back (`.back`). Works while the level plays. Not done: dragging a gun out of its circle (a circle's press opens the gun), slot-to-slot moves. Test: `tests/browser/auto-drag.test.js`. Pictures: `node tools/dragshots.js` (`tests/build/autoshots/drag-*.png`).

**Stage 9:** **Gun arc:** a player's helmet at the top of the nav (`holdHelm`) opens his menu on **release** (a tap), not on press; held `DEV.autoHoldMs` (350) his 4 guns fan out above it (`GunArc`, `.aarc`/`.aarcc[data-arc]`, the nav's gun circles from `navRow` at `guns`; positions `auto/nav.js` `gunArc`, radius `DEV.autoArcR` 110, kept 30 px off the screen's edges); sliding onto a gun lights it (`.drop`, `arcPick` within 30 px; empty ones never light); letting go there calls `setActive` (saved), anywhere else cancels. Tests that tap a helmet with `dispatchEvent('pointerdown')` must send a `pointerup` too (on `window`: `new PointerEvent('pointerup')`). **Scrap:** a bag item (not gold) dragged onto the gold tile lights it (`.drop`, `DropAt.to = 'scrap'`) and `scrapAt` turns it into gold (saved) with a `CoinBurst` (`.acoins`: 9 coins and "+n", CSS, 0.9 s, `SFX.ui('coin')`). **Leave on the ground:** a bag item dragged onto the play area (`.aplay.drop`, `to: 'play'`) leaves the bag (saved) and lies on the ground in front of the team: `art/titlescene.js` `leaveItem` (an `S.loot` pickup with `left: true`: never vacuumed, not waited for by LEVEL CLEARED; in a level it scrolls off as any left-behind drop). Knobs Dev → Auto → "Auto: bag". Test: `tests/browser/auto-stage9.test.js`. Pictures: `node tools/arcshots.js` (`tests/build/autoshots/arc-*.png`).

**Stage 10a (`throwPress`):** in the hub only, a press on the grab handle of a gold, red or green stack is a throw, not a drag (in a level the old drag stands). Moved past `MOVE` it's a swipe: let go at ≥ `DEV.autoFlickMin` px/s (the last 100 ms of the trail) → one out (`spend`, saved) at the finger (clamped into the play area: `world` via the scene's camera ref `cam` and `camAt`) with the finger's velocity × `autoFlickK` (`screenToWorldVel`). Held still (`autoStreamWait`) away from the stack → a stream (a 33 ms timer): the rate ramps `autoStreamRate0` → `autoStreamRate1` over `autoStreamRamp` s, speed = px moved × `autoStreamK` (world/s) in the moved direction. (The whole-stack lump, held still on the stack, was removed in feedback round 1: it caught the start of a spray.) The 200 ms timer drains `H.back` into the bag; leaving the hub puts anything still thrown back. Thrown things are drawn by `game/render/titledraw.js` `drawThrown` (over the machines). Pictures: `node tools/throwshots.js` (`throw-*.png`).
**Stage 12 (extra players):** at the players level each empty circle carries `data-pslot`; a green gem from the bag over one lights it (`.drop`, `DropAt.to = 'player'`, while the team is under `MAX_PLAYERS`; anything else is refused). In the hub a green press starts as a throw (`throwPress`), and turns into a drag (ghost, lit circle, `drop`) as soon as the finger is over an empty circle. Let go there: `addPlayer(run)` (a green spent, the starter gun, a Buzzsaw into the bag; saved) and `auto/addrunner.js` `sceneAddRunner` (he teleports in beside the team, hub or level). The camera stays on player 1 in the hub (the others walk in a line behind him, so they are framed with him); the level's view holds the whole team anyway. Test hook: `window.__autoHub` (the hub scene under `__TEST_TITLE`). Test: `tests/browser/auto-drag.test.js` (end). Pictures: `node tools/teamshots.js` (`team-*.png`).
- **Stage 10b (payouts):** the hub is made with `run.paid` (`hubScene`'s 5th argument; created on the run if missing) and `S.hub.fits` / `S.hub.take` (`bagFits` / `bagAdd`, saved, redrawn): a machine's payout flies into the bag (auto/payout.js). The 200 ms loop saves the run when `H.paidV` changes. Leaving the hub, payouts still out (`S.loot`) go into the bag.

**Stage 13 (save, resume, cleanup):** the run is saved on every move, every hub payout or return, each hub⇄level swap, and now also whenever a drop goes into the bag in a level (the 200 ms loop watches `L.bagV`), so a quit mid-level reopens in the hub (`where` starts at hub) with the bag kept. ⏸ → **New run** (twice: `PauseMenu` `onNew` → `freshRun`: a `newRun()` saved over the run, then a reload to the title). The old game (`ui/app.js` `App`, the level worker `game/levelgen.js`) is no longer in the build: `index.html` 1,594,144 → 1,192,534 bytes; its 91 browser suites moved to `tests/retired/` (same depth as `tests/browser/`, so a merge back is a move). Test: `tests/browser/auto-save.test.js`.
