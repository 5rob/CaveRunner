# ui/auto/ — layer 6: CaveRunner Auto's screens (branch `autobattler`)

React for the auto-battler (`AUTOBATTLER.md`). The pure parts are in `src/auto/` (the run model, the save);
this folder only shows them and takes the taps.

## Rules

- Phone first (412×880, touch); every gesture works with a mouse too.
- The title menu's colours (`.titlemenu`, `.tslot`: dark purple panels, orange edge), not new ones.
- Slots in scrolling areas are `pan-y`; anything you drag gets `touch-action: none` (`.grab`, as the old Bag).
- No game logic here: moves on the run go through `src/auto/run.js`.

**Path blocked (stage 5b):** in a level, when no gun in play can clear the rock ahead (`auto/clear.js`), the pilot stops the team and `AutoScreen` shows `.ablocked` ("Path blocked", pulsing, `style.css`) over the play area; it reads `levelState(S).blocked` every 200 ms. Under `__TEST_TITLE` the level scene is on `window.__autoScene` (for `tools/clearshots.js`).

**LEVEL CLEARED (stage 7):** `runScene`'s `over` paints `art/cleared.js` `clearedText` while `levelClearedAge(S)` ≥ 0 (top at 14% of the play area): the title's letters (`titleLetter`) drop in one by one with bounces, a spark burst and a ring at each landing, a shine sweep, the title's bob, a fade over the last 0.5 s. Knobs Dev → Auto → "Auto: level" (`autoClearT`, `autoClearWait`). Pictures: `tools/clearedshots.js`.

**Enemies (stage 6 part 1):** the level gets `run.tier` (`levelScene`'s 6th argument). While the arena's boss lives, `AutoScreen` shows `.abossbar` (its name and a red bar, `style.css`) across the top of the play area, from `auto/enemies.js` `levelBoss(S)`, polled with the blocked hint (200 ms). Every player fallen (`levelLost(S)`): `next` takes the team home with `levelFailed(run)` (healed, tier unchanged) and saves.
- `icons.js` (stage 8a, owner rounds): `PixIcon` (pixel maps `NAV_ICONS`: the menu's guns/exo/perks/stats, the exo kinds hp/speed/jet/carrot; also the bag's exo mods), `HelmetIcon` (a player's helmet, `art/sprites.js` `helmetIcon` through `pixelSprite`), `GlyphIcon` (a mod's/perk's/gold's/gem's glyph: drawn big, its painted pixels found, scaled to fill a `GLYPH_PX` grid, alpha thresholded; `.apixg` 76% of the tile, `image-rendering: pixelated`, no glow). No emoji in the nav (owner). `NAV_ICONS` and `tintShade` live in `art/itemicon.js` (re-exported here) so the canvas can draw them too.
- **Pickups fly into the bag** (owner): the scene calls `S.onTake(it, x, y)` when a pickup goes in (hub and level, `titlescene.js stepLoot`); `AutoScreen`'s `flyIn` finds the slot (`auto/run.js bagSlotOf`) and the screen point (`camTo`), and `FlyTile` (`.afly`, the bag tile's look) flies there in `FLY_MS` (Web Animations), eased, from 0.55× size (a new slot shows empty till it lands: `fresh`); a slot scrolled out of sight: the bag's nearest edge; no bag on show: no flight. The tile outline rule is `auto/itemtile.js itemEdgeCol` (shared with the canvas). Tests: `tests/logic/auto-flyin`, `tests/browser/auto-flyin`; pictures `tools/vendiconshots.js`.

**Drag and drop (stage 8b):** a bag tile or a nav slot tile takes a press (`down` in `AutoScreen`, window pointer listeners, mouse and touch alike). Only the **grab handle** `.agrab` (a circle round the icon's centre, radius `DEV.autoGrab` × the tile width, Dev → Auto → "Auto: bag", default 0.35) is `touch-action: none`: a press there that moves past 6 px (`MOVE`) is a drag; anywhere else the tile stays `pan-y` (the bag scrolls; a nav slot row `pan-x`) and the browser's pointercancel ends the press with no card. A press released within 6 px is a **tap**: the item's card (`CardPop`, portalled into the body: `ModCard`/`GunCard`/`PerkCard` from `ui/cards.js`, an exo mod's own `ExoCard` from `STAT_PERKS`; gold and gems have none; the shade closes it). While dragging: the `.aghost` tile above the finger, the source faded (`.lift`), a target it fits lit green (`.drop`): bag → a gun's mod slots (nav at gun, mods), the 4 gun circles (nav at guns, `data-gslot`, guns), a category's slots (nav at cat, its exo mods only), perk slots (nav at perks, non-stat perks); a nav slot → the bag (unfit). The move goes through `auto/run.js` (`fitMod` … `unfitPerk`) and saves; anything else springs back (`.back`). Works while the level plays. Not done: dragging a gun out of its circle (a circle's press opens the gun), slot-to-slot moves. Test: `tests/browser/auto-drag.test.js`. Pictures: `node tools/dragshots.js` (`tests/build/autoshots/drag-*.png`).

**Stage 9:** **Gun arc:** a player's helmet at the top of the nav (`holdHelm`) opens his menu on **release** (a tap), not on press; held `DEV.autoHoldMs` (350) his 4 guns fan out above it (`GunArc`, `.aarc`/`.aarcc[data-arc]`, the nav's gun circles from `navRow` at `guns`; positions `auto/nav.js` `gunArc`, radius `DEV.autoArcR` 110, kept 30 px off the screen's edges); sliding onto a gun lights it (`.drop`, `arcPick` within 30 px; empty ones never light); letting go there calls `setActive` (saved), anywhere else cancels. **In a level** (feedback round 2) the hold instead puts that player in manual mode (`C.lock` = him, `levelControl`: the pill stick steers him, auto/level.js `roamStep` moves the view; release does nothing more); B lets him go. The arc stays in the hub (player 1 is always steered there). **Stage 10a (`throwPress`, removed in feedback round 2):** in the hub only, a press on the grab handle of a gold, red or green stack is a throw, not a drag (in a level the old drag stands). Moved past `MOVE` it's a swipe: let go at ≥ `DEV.autoFlickMin` px/s (the last 100 ms of the trail) → one out (`spend`, saved) at the finger (clamped into the play area: `world` via the scene's camera ref `cam` and `camAt`) with the finger's velocity × `autoFlickK` (`screenToWorldVel`). Held still (`autoStreamWait`) away from the stack → a stream (a 33 ms timer): the rate ramps `autoStreamRate0` → `autoStreamRate1` over `autoStreamRamp` s, speed = px moved × `autoStreamK` (world/s) in the moved direction. (The whole-stack lump, held still on the stack, was removed in feedback round 1: it caught the start of a spray.) The 200 ms timer drains `H.back` into the bag; leaving the hub puts anything still thrown back. Thrown things are drawn by `game/render/titledraw.js` `drawThrown` (over the machines). Pictures: `node tools/throwshots.js` (`throw-*.png`).
**Stage 12 (extra players):** at the players level each empty circle carries `data-pslot`; a green gem from the bag over one lights it (`.drop`, `DropAt.to = 'player'`, while the team is under `MAX_PLAYERS`; anything else is refused). In the hub a green press starts as a throw (`throwPress`), and turns into a drag (ghost, lit circle, `drop`) as soon as the finger is over an empty circle. Let go there: `addPlayer(run)` (a green spent, the starter gun, a Buzzsaw into the bag; saved) and `auto/addrunner.js` `sceneAddRunner` (he teleports in beside the team, hub or level). The camera stays on player 1 in the hub (the others walk in a line behind him, so they are framed with him); the level's view holds the whole team anyway. Test hook: `window.__autoHub` (the hub scene under `__TEST_TITLE`). Test: `tests/browser/auto-drag.test.js` (end). Pictures: `node tools/teamshots.js` (`team-*.png`).
- **Stage 10b (payouts):** the hub is made with `run.paid` (`hubScene`'s 5th argument; created on the run if missing) and `S.hub.fits` / `S.hub.take` (`bagFits` / `bagAdd`, saved, redrawn): a machine's payout flies into the bag (auto/payout.js). The 200 ms loop saves the run when `H.paidV` changes. Leaving the hub, payouts still out (`S.loot`) go into the bag.

**Stage 13 (save, resume, cleanup):** the run is saved on every move, every hub payout or return, each hub⇄level swap, and now also whenever a drop goes into the bag in a level (the 200 ms loop watches `L.bagV`), so a quit mid-level reopens in the hub (`where` starts at hub) with the bag kept. ⏸ → **New run** (twice: `PauseMenu` `onNew` → `freshRun`: a `newRun()` saved over the run, then a reload to the title). The old game (`ui/app.js` `App`, the level worker `game/levelgen.js`) is no longer in the build: `index.html` 1,594,144 → 1,192,534 bytes; its 91 browser suites moved to `tests/retired/` (same depth as `tests/browser/`, so a merge back is a move). Test: `tests/browser/auto-save.test.js`.
